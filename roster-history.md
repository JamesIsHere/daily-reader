# Roster history — people, dates, verdicts

The editorial ledger. roster.md is the machine source of truth (outlets,
feeds, what gets polled); this file is the person-level record of who is
followed, since when, who was cut, when, and a short why. Everyone on the
list is permanently under review — presence here is a standing decision,
not a tenure.

Contract: every creator add or removal in roster.md gets a dated line
here, same edit, same session. Reasons stay short — one clause, not a
paragraph.

Not rendered anywhere yet (deliberate, 2026-08-01). Future home: the
footer of the rolling view / newsletter — "what James is reading, and
what he stopped reading" — so the answer never has to be repeated by
hand.
