# Daily Reader on Your iPhone

This puts a small news feed called Daily Reader on your iPhone home
screen, like an app. It takes about 30 seconds. There is nothing to
install, nothing to sign up for, and nothing to pay. It updates itself.

## The link

https://YOUR-MIRROR.vercel.app

(Substitute the real mirror URL — projectName in
publish\.vercel\project.json, gitignored — when regenerating the PDF
for a recipient. The URL stays out of tracked files by design.)

## Directions

1. On your iPhone, tap the link above. It should open in Safari, the
   blue compass icon that comes with every iPhone. If it opens
   somewhere else, press and hold the link, choose Copy, then open
   Safari yourself and paste the address at the top.

2. Tap the Share button. That is the small square with an arrow
   pointing up, in the middle at the bottom of the screen.

3. Scroll down the menu that pops up and tap "Add to Home Screen."

4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner.

That is it. A dark blue icon that says DR is now on your home screen.

## Using it

Tap the DR icon any time to read the latest. The feed refreshes on its
own several times a day, and the line at the top always shows when it
was last updated. You never have to update the app, log in, or do
anything else.

## If something goes wrong

If you do not see "Add to Home Screen" in step 3, you are almost
certainly not in Safari. Copy the link, open Safari, paste it in the
address bar, and start again from step 2.
